System integrations

We connect e-shops, accounting software, couriers, payments, CRMs and other systems through their APIs.

Discuss a project Price: from €500 · Timeline: 1–4 weeks

For example, we can connect

E-shop ↔ accounting

WooCommerce or Shopify orders turn into invoices in your accounting software automatically, and stock levels flow back.

Automatic shipping labels

Omniva, DPD or LP Express labels created from an order in one click, or fully automatically.

Payments

Montonio, Paysera or Stripe payments that update the order status and send the client an invoice.

Enquiry form → CRM

An enquiry from your website lands in your CRM or spreadsheet, and the client gets a confirmation email straight away.

Data sync

Products, prices and stock between supplier, warehouse and shop, updated on a schedule.

An API for your system

If your system needs an API so partners or other tools can connect to it, we design and build it.

Price and timeline

What you get

  • A review of your current tools and what their APIs allow
  • A working integration with an error log
  • Alerts when something fails
  • Documentation of how the integration works
  • Code that belongs to you
  • User guides, plus video guides if needed
  • A 3-month warranty after launch

Price: from €500

A fixed price, agreed in writing before we start. The exact amount depends on the scope and integrations.

Timeline: 1–4 weeks

We give the exact timeline in the plan, together with the price, before any work starts. If a bug caused by our work shows up within 3 months of launch, we fix it free of charge. After that, if you need it, we can keep maintaining and developing the system.
