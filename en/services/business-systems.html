A business management system built around your process

When orders, clients and stock are scattered across spreadsheets, emails and chats, we build one system where everything is visible at once. It fits the way you work, not the other way round.

Discuss a project Price: from €2,500 · Timeline: 4–10 weeks
01 —

Who it's for

Manual work takes too much time

You enter the same data into several spreadsheets and hunt for answers in files and chats. A system does it for you.

You need features no app offers

Your process needs its own steps, calculations or integrations that Pipedrive or Trello don't offer. We build the system around your work.

Work stops without one person

When they're on holiday, nobody can find what they need. In a system, everyone sees everything.

You're growing, but processes can't keep up

More clients means more chaos. A system lets you grow without hiring extra people.

03 —

Price and timeline

What you get

  • A system that runs in the browser on desktop and mobile
  • Users with different permissions (manager, staff, client)
  • Your data moved over from spreadsheets or the tools you use now
  • Integrations with the tools you already use, if needed
  • Code that belongs to you
  • User guides, plus video guides if needed
  • A 3-month warranty after launch

Price: from €2,500

A fixed price, agreed in writing before we start. The exact amount depends on the scope and integrations.

Timeline: 4–10 weeks

We give the exact timeline in the plan, together with the price, before any work starts. If a bug caused by our work shows up within 3 months of launch, we fix it free of charge. After that, if you need it, we can keep maintaining and developing the system.
