About me

In my free time I'm building an automated system for creating and publishing short vertical videos. An AI agent that turns an idea into a video – script, scenes, voice-over and subtitles – already works. Its output is checked by code, and a human makes the key decisions. The agent is also my final thesis at Vilnius University of Applied Sciences.

I've always enjoyed automating things: before my first commercial job I wrote Python scripts that collected flat and car listing data for analysis. I've been programming for about 2.5 years (React, TypeScript, C# .NET). I build systems for clients, from working out the requirements to launch. Before IT I worked as a transport planner at Girteka, so I understand working with KPIs and across departments.
